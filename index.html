About SPPU NEP-2020 CBCS Pattern

Savitribai Phule Pune University has implemented the Choice Based Credit System under the National Education Policy (NEP) for T.Y. B.Sc. (Computer Science).

Industry-Aligned Core

The curriculum shifts to modern frameworks including Angular, React, Node.js, Spring Boot, Artificial Intelligence, and Cloud-native NoSQL architectures.

Electives & VSC Tracks

Flexible career specialization in Data Science, Embedded Systems & IoT, Android Development, Agile Scrum practices, and Software Quality Automation.

Evaluation Architecture

Each 2-credit course comprises Internal Evaluation (IE: 15 marks) and University Examination (UE: 35 marks) with rigorous hands-on laboratory assessment.
